I work with industrial partners to turn methods developed for demanding scientific environments into practical AI systems. The focus is on domain-specific models that can run close to the instrument, work with limited or unusual data, and deliver results that can be validated in the physical world.

{{< application-principles >}}

Methods developed for particle detectors can transfer to **tomography, industrial imaging, and other sparse sensor systems**. The aim is fast, high-fidelity 3D reconstruction that turns raw measurements into useful information on site.

{{< application-case

Large vision models can be distilled and optimized for **robots, drones, and embedded systems**. Running perception locally reduces latency, power use, and dependence on cloud infrastructure while keeping sensitive data under the partner's control.
{{< /application-case >}}

{{< application-case

    title="Generative materials discovery"

Property-guided generative models can propose materials against partner-defined constraints. Coupled with laboratory validation, this creates a closed loop for exploring candidates for **magnets, energy storage, catalysts, and other advanced materials**.
